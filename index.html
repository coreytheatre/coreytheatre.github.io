Short Bio

Corey Theatre is a Gunditjmara-Belgian singer-songwriter and fingerstyle guitarist who performs in English and various Aboriginal languages, from fluently spoken Pitjantjatjara to revival languages like Gunditjmara and Kaurna. His eclectic guitar playing draws on British and Scandinavian folk traditions, ragtime and blues. His debut album, Ngathuk Ngalina, saw Theatre exploring music as a medium for reviving the Gunditjmara language and features a title-track duet with Uncle Archie Roach. Corey’s songwriting bridges diverse worldviews; his track "Pang Kayap" blends Christian themes with traditional Kulin creation stories, while "Mikawamangka" uses the Kaurna language to embed observations of Country directly into song. Drawing from his landmark releases and an expanding repertoire of unreleased songs, Corey’s live performances offer a captivating, cross-cultural storytelling experience. His highly acclaimed work has earned him an Australia’s Got Language Talent national win alongside multiple South Australian Music Award nominations.

“I like what he’s doing with language, you know, reclaiming language, singing songs in the language”
— Archie Roach

Workshop: Reviving Language Through Song

As a leading proponent of Gunditjmara language revival and expert in the Aboriginal languages of Victoria, Dr Corey Theatre can deliver workshops discussing the languages and linguistics of Victoria and how he uses music as a medium to revive and preserve Aboriginal languages. The session will draw on yarning methodologies, creating an informal and interactive opportunity to learn about song-craft in the context of language reclamation.
